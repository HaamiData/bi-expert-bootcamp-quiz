# Question 6: Characteristics of a Good KPI

**Topics:** KPI, Key Performance Indicators, Strategic Goals

## 📝 Question

> Which option describes the characteristics of a good KPI?

## 🔢 Options

**Option 1:** A metric that is calculated monthly and manually

**Option 2:** A metric whose value is always positive and can never become negative

**Option 3:** A metric that is measurable, related to a strategic goal and actionable

**Option 4:** A comprehensive collection of all operational metrics to be seen on one dashboard

## 💡 About this question

A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) must support decision-making. Its main characteristics:

- It is **measurable** (clear definition and formula).
- It is **tied to a strategic goal**.
- It is **actionable**: seeing it, we know what to do.

A KPI is not defined by "all metrics" or by a “monthly/manual calculation method.”

## 🔍 Detailed review of every option

### Option 1 — ❌ Incorrect

> A metric that is calculated monthly and manually

**Incorrect.** Being manual and monthly is not a virtue and has nothing to do with KPI quality. A good KPI is usually computed automatically at a frequency that fits the decision; manual work adds errors and delay.

### Option 2 — ❌ Incorrect

> A metric whose value is always positive and can never become negative

**Incorrect.** Always being positive is not a required property of a KPI. Many valid KPIs can be negative (such as sales growth or net profit). The criterion is connection to a goal and actionability, not the sign of the value.

### Option 3 — ✅ Correct

> A metric that is measurable, related to a strategic goal and actionable

**Correct.** A good KPI is measurable, tied to a strategic goal and actionable; that is, its result leads to a specific decision and action.

### Option 4 — ❌ Incorrect

> A comprehensive collection of all operational metrics to be seen on one dashboard

**Incorrect.** Putting all operational metrics on one dashboard creates clutter and makes prioritization impossible. A KPI should be **selective and important**, not a comprehensive list of all metrics.

## ✅ Correct answer

**Option 3**

> A metric that is measurable, related to a strategic goal and actionable

## 🎯 Why it is correct

A KPI exists for decisions: it must be measurable, linked to a strategic goal and actionable. Option 3 says exactly that.

## 📌 Key takeaway

A good KPI = measurable + goal-related + actionable (and few in number).
