# Question 15: Semi-Additive Measures

**Topics:** Additivity, Measures, Balances, Dimensional Modeling

## 📝 Question

> Which of the following is a Semi-Additive Measure?

## 🔢 Options

**Option 1:** Profit margin as a percentage

**Option 2:** Bank account balance

**Option 3:** Discount percentage

**Option 4:** Sales amount

## 💡 About this question

Measures come in three kinds regarding summability:

- **Additive:** can be summed across all dimensions (such as sales amount).
- **Semi-additive:** can be summed across some dimensions but **not across time** (such as balances; you cannot add the balances of two days, you take the last value or an average).
- **Non-additive:** cannot be summed across any dimension (such as ratios and percentages).

## 🔍 Detailed review of every option

### Option 1 — ❌ Incorrect

> Profit margin as a percentage

**Incorrect.** Profit margin (a percentage) is **non-additive**; summing percentages is meaningless and it must be computed as a ratio of sums.

### Option 2 — ✅ Correct

> Bank account balance

**Correct.** An account balance can be summed across accounts (customers) but not across time (today's balance + yesterday's balance is meaningless); so it is semi-additive.

### Option 3 — ❌ Incorrect

> Discount percentage

**Incorrect.** Discount percentage is non-additive; summing or simply averaging percentages is misleading.

### Option 4 — ❌ Incorrect

> Sales amount

**Incorrect.** Sales amount is **additive** and can be summed across all dimensions (time, region, product).

## ✅ Correct answer

**Option 2**

> Bank account balance

## 🎯 Why it is correct

A balance cannot be summed over the time dimension but can over the others; hence semi-additive.

## 📌 Key takeaway

Sales amount: additive; balance: semi-additive; percentage/ratio: non-additive.
