# Question 16: Type of Analysis: Diagnostic

**Topics:** Types of Analytics, Descriptive, Diagnostic, Predictive, Prescriptive

## 📝 Question

> To which type of analysis does the question “Why did sales in the northern region decrease last quarter?” belong?

## 🔢 Options

**Option 1:** Prescriptive

**Option 2:** Predictive

**Option 3:** Descriptive

**Option 4:** Diagnostic

## 💡 About this question

Know the four levels of analytics by their core question:

- **Descriptive:** what happened?
- **Diagnostic:** **why** did it happen?
- **Predictive:** what will happen?
- **Prescriptive:** what should we do?

The question starts with "why" and asks for the reason behind a past event.

## 🔍 Detailed review of every option

### Option 1 — ❌ Incorrect

> Prescriptive

**Incorrect.** Prescriptive means recommending action ("what should we do to make up for the drop?"), not finding causes.

### Option 2 — ❌ Incorrect

> Predictive

**Incorrect.** Predictive is about the future ("what will next quarter's sales be?"), whereas the question is about a past decline.

### Option 3 — ❌ Incorrect

> Descriptive

**Incorrect.** Descriptive only says what happened ("how much did northern sales fall?") and does not address the cause.

### Option 4 — ✅ Correct

> Diagnostic

**Correct.** "Why did it decrease?" is root-cause analysis and belongs to Diagnostic analytics; it is usually done via drill-down and comparing contributing factors.

## ✅ Correct answer

**Option 4**

> Diagnostic

## 🎯 Why it is correct

A "why" question = root-cause analysis = Diagnostic analytics.

## 📌 Key takeaway

What happened? Descriptive | Why? Diagnostic | What will happen? Predictive | What should we do? Prescriptive.
