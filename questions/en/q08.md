# Question 8: Sales Growth Percentage

**Topics:** Growth Percentage Calculation, Base of Comparison

## 📝 Question

> If sales go from 80 million tomans in the first month to 100 million tomans in the second month, what is the sales growth percentage?

## 🔢 Options

**Option 1:** 20 percent

**Option 2:** 80 percent

**Option 3:** 30 percent

**Option 4:** 25 percent

## 💡 About this question

Growth percentage is measured relative to the **starting value (base)**:

`growth = (new value − old value) / old value × 100`

`(100 − 80) / 80 × 100 = 20 / 80 × 100 = 25%`

## 🔍 Detailed review of every option

### Option 1 — ❌ Incorrect

> 20 percent

**Incorrect.** 20 million is the absolute increase. Dividing 20 by 100 (the new value) gives 20%, but the base for growth must be the **first month's** value, i.e. 80.

### Option 2 — ❌ Incorrect

> 80 percent

**Incorrect.** 80% is the ratio of the old to the new value (`80/100`), not growth. The growth is 20 million, not 80 percent.

### Option 3 — ❌ Incorrect

> 30 percent

**Incorrect.** 30% does not follow from these numbers by any correct method. A 20-million increase on a base of 80 million is 25%.

### Option 4 — ✅ Correct

> 25 percent

**Correct.** `(100 − 80) / 80 = 0.25`, i.e. sales grew by 25%.

## ✅ Correct answer

**Option 4**

> 25 percent

## 🎯 Why it is correct

Growth must be computed on the initial value: `20 ÷ 80 = 25%`.

## 📌 Key takeaway

The denominator of a growth percentage is always the earlier period's value (the base).
